# User Guide — NHI Inventory Management System

A quick tour of the main modules. Sign in with the credentials listed in the
[README](../README.md#login-information).

## Dashboard

The landing screen after login. Shows key figures (sales, stock, dues, recent activity) and
quick access to common actions. Use the left navigation to move between modules.

## Point of Sale (POS)

- Scan barcodes with a USB scanner or the built-in **webcam scanner**.
- Search and add products by name/code; adjust quantity and price per line.
- Choose the payment method; split or partial payments are supported.
- **Hold** a sale and **resume** it later from the held-sales list.
- Print or reprint receipts; configure receipt layout in **Settings → POS Print Settings**.

## Inventory

- Manage **products**, **categories**, **brands** and **units**.
- **Stock adjustments** let you correct quantities with a reason.
- Generate and print **barcode label sheets** for products.

## Purchases

- Record purchases from suppliers; stock and supplier ledgers update automatically.
- **Purchase returns** reverse stock and ledger entries for returned goods.

## Sales

- Browse **sales history**, open a sale to view its details, and **reprint** receipts.
- **Sales returns** reverse stock and customer ledger entries.

## Khata (Customer & Supplier Ledgers)

- View running **customer** and **supplier** ledgers (khata) with opening/closing balances.
- **Receive payments** (from customers) and **pay suppliers**; dues are tracked per party.
- Send payment reminders / messages and review the **message history** (WhatsApp).

## Expenses

- Record day-to-day expenses against categories (electricity, rent, salary, transport, …).

## Employees

- Maintain employee records, mark **attendance** and **work days**, **post salaries**, and
  review each employee's ledger and dues.

## Daily Closing

- Produce an end-of-day summary of cash and sales, and review previous closings.

## Reports

- Profit and operational reports with date filtering.
- Export reports to **Excel** for further analysis.

## Settings & Administration

- **Company & shop settings**, themes (light/dark), and language (English / Urdu RTL).
- **POS print settings** and **print layout** configuration.
- **User management** (admins can add/edit users) and **Shop management** (multi-shop).
- **Backup & Restore** (admin only) — see the
  [README](../README.md#data-storage--backup) for backup steps.
- **Audit / activity log** for administrative traceability.

## Tips

- Always create a **backup** before updating the application or making large data changes.
- Change the default administrator password right after the first sign-in.
- Keep the application window's data safe by not copying the database while the app is running.
